A different standard

Large matchmaking firms and boutique ones can work very differently. This is what you get here.

At many large services

  • You speak to a salesperson first, then a rotating team.
  • Matches often come from whoever is available.
  • You may be left to plan the date yourself.
  • Women in the database are called when a client needs candidates.

At Human Matched

  • You work with Jessica from the first call to the last debrief.
  • You meet only people who fit what both of you asked for.
  • The venue is chosen and booked, and you both get a short note.
  • Every woman is interviewed, heard, and asked before each introduction.

How it works

Men engage me for a private search. Women join the network free. Nobody browses profiles, and nobody browses yours.

  1. Get in touch

    Men request a first call. Women complete an application that takes about eight minutes.

  2. We talk on video

    A real conversation, in English or Spanish, so I hear what you want in your own words.

  3. I look for someone who fits

    I search for a person who meets what you asked for. If nobody does yet, I don't send anybody.

  4. You both say yes

    I describe the person and tell you why I chose them. Either of you can decline without giving a reason.

  5. I arrange the date

    I pick the place, book it, and send you both a short note. First dates are drinks or coffee.

  6. We talk afterwards

    I call each of you the next day. What you tell me shapes the next introduction.

What you can count on

Your limits are respected

If you tell me something is a dealbreaker, you will not be introduced to someone who breaks it. That goes for women and for men.

One introduction at a time

No swiping and no endless options. One person, chosen for a reason I can explain to you.

Everyone has been interviewed

I meet every person on video before they join. Every man's identity and background are checked before a woman hears his name.

Discretion

No public profiles, and I never pass your name to another matchmaker. You can leave at any time and I will delete what I hold.

Why I started this

I've been the friend who sets people up for 12 years. It started with my own circle. More than 20 introductions later, three of those couples are married, and counting. I'm a good listener, I care about how people feel, and I love the moment two people click.

Before I got married, matchmakers used to reach out to me all the time for their clients. They'd pass my name around, and there was always someone they wanted me to meet, but it was never anyone close to what I'd told them I was looking for. I felt like a product on a shelf, not a person.

I kept thinking I could do this better, and that I could help far more people than my own circle. Today I'm happily married, and I want other people to have what I have. So now I do this full time, and I listen to the women as closely as the men.
