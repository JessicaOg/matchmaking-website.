How it works

Joining is free for women. There are no profiles to browse and nobody browsing yours.

  1. Apply

    Tell me about your life and what you want. It takes about eight minutes.

  2. We talk on video

    A 45-minute conversation, in English or Spanish, so I hear it in your own words.

  3. I look for someone who fits

    You hear from me only when a person meets what you asked for. If nobody does, I don't send anybody.

  4. You say yes or no

    I describe him and tell you why I chose him. You can decline without giving a reason.

  5. I arrange the date

    I pick the place, book it, and send you both a short note. First dates are drinks or coffee.

  6. We talk afterwards

    I call each of you the next day. What you tell me shapes the next introduction.

What you can count on

Your limits are respected

If you tell me something is a dealbreaker, you will not be introduced to someone who breaks it. That goes for women and for men.

One introduction at a time

No swiping and no endless options. One person, chosen for a reason I can explain to you.

Everyone has been interviewed

I meet every person on video before they join. Every man's identity and background are checked before you hear his name.

Your details stay with me

No public profiles, and I never pass your name to another matchmaker. You can leave at any time and I will delete what I hold.

A network that looks like the world

Many matchmaking services serve a narrow slice of people. This one is built for every background from the start.

Technology in the background

A matching system keeps track of what everyone asked for, so nothing gets missed. A person makes every decision.

Why I started this

I've always been the friend who sets people up. It started with my own friends, and three of the couples I introduced are married now. I'm a good listener, I care about how people feel, and I love the moment two people click.

Before I got married, matchmakers used to reach out to me all the time for their clients. They'd pass my name around, and there was always someone they wanted me to meet, but it was never anyone close to what I'd told them I was looking for. I felt like a product on a shelf, not a person.

I kept thinking I could do this better, and that I could help far more people than my own circle. Today I'm happily married, and I want other people to have what I have. So now I do this full time, and I listen to the women as closely as the men.

Jessica

Founder, Human Matched
